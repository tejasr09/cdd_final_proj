# Hypothesis personas

> **HYPOTHESIS — to be validated.** These are planning prompts inferred from the project brief, not research findings or validated personas. Replace or revise them only after genuine participant research. No quotes, demographics, or observed behaviors are asserted here.

## H1 — Operations coordinator

- **Hypothesized responsibility:** register operational events, maintain dependency information, run live analyses, and coordinate responses.
- **Hypothesized need:** quickly inspect which modeled entities may be affected and why.
- **Questions to validate:** What information is needed before acting? Which event states and approvals match the real workflow? How are dependency edits reviewed?
- **Risk if wrong:** the product may add steps without helping coordination.

## H2 — Department coordinator

- **Hypothesized responsibility:** inspect local consequences and help validate dependencies relevant to a department.
- **Hypothesized need:** understand inbound/outbound relationships and identify stale or uncertain data.
- **Questions to validate:** What should this role be able to edit versus propose? How are relationship owners identified? What level of organization-wide visibility is appropriate?
- **Risk if wrong:** read-only visibility may be insufficient, or broader access may expose data unnecessarily.

## H3 — Analyst

- **Hypothesized responsibility:** explore hypothetical events/interventions and compare scenario results with a baseline.
- **Hypothesized need:** inspect assumptions, alternative paths, confidence meaning, and changes between runs.
- **Questions to validate:** Which comparisons support a decision? How should uncertainty and incomplete runs be presented? What provenance is required?
- **Risk if wrong:** a scenario interface may appear more authoritative than its inputs justify.

## H4 — Person affected by an operational change

- **Hypothesized responsibility:** experience a possible consequence, such as a schedule or transport disruption.
- **Hypothesized need:** receive an understandable explanation of what may change and what remains uncertain.
- **Questions to validate:** What information is useful to affected people? Should this role use the application directly, or receive information through a coordinator?
- **Risk if wrong:** the product may optimize for administrators while overlooking affected people, or expose them to a tool they do not need.
